### Executive Profile

Multidisciplinary Full-Stack Engineer and 3D Creative Technologist with over 6 years of commercial experience engineering high-performance web systems, real-time 3D web applications, and generative AI pipelines. Founder of **Hi-Viz Studios**, directing digital content, CGI, and visual experiences with over 2M+ organic impressions.

Specializing in bridging the gap between heavy technical engineering (**PyTorch, FastAPI, Next.js, Node.js**) and immersive spatial visuals (**Three.js, WebGL, React Three Fiber, GSAP, Blender, Unreal Engine**).

- **Commercial Delivery:** 50+ deployed client platforms and 30+ interactive 3D WebGL web experiences.
- **AI & Computer Vision:** Real-time garment matting, anatomical pose locking, and latent diffusion pipelines.
- **Spatial Computing & VFX:** Procedural shader development, physics simulations, and 3D web optimization.
